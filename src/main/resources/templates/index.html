Privacy by Architecture

            

            
        

        

            
We believe your documents belong to you. Here is how our architecture guarantees your privacy:

            

                

                    1. Pure In-Memory Processing
                    
Uploaded documents are streamed directly into isolated RAM buffers for manipulation and are never written to disk or permanent databases.

                

                

                    2. 30-Minute Automatic Purge
                    
Generated download files are assigned random UUID tokens and are permanently purged by an automated background cleaner after 30 minutes.

                

                

                    3. Zero Document Mining or Selling
                    
We do not train models on your files, track document text, inspect personal information, or share data with advertisers.

                

                

                    4. Local & Private AI Engine
                    
Our document summarizer and Q&A run on-premise using an embedded offline NLP engine, ensuring no document text leaves the server unless an external API key is explicitly configured.
